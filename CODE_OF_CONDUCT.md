# A good place to build together

OpenPali works with records about a community recovering from a disaster.
Bring care for the people behind the parcels, and for the people building
this project.

## How we work

- Welcome people with different backgrounds, abilities, and levels of experience.
- Be direct about ideas and kind to the person offering them.
- Give useful context, share evidence, and make room for corrections.
- Respect privacy. Keep personal contact details and private conversations
  out of issues, pull requests, screenshots, and datasets.
- Discuss what the public record supports without judging residents,
  property owners, or their pace of recovery.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexual harassment, doxxing,
and persistent unwanted contact are unacceptable. These expectations apply
to the repository and other spaces maintained for the project.

## When something goes wrong

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, close disruptive threads,
or restrict participation to keep collaboration constructive. A correction
or disagreement is welcome; abuse is not.

For abuse on GitHub, use GitHub's **Report content** or **Report abuse**
controls. GitHub explains the available reporting routes in
[Reporting abuse or spam](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).
If GitHub offers reporting to this repository's maintainers, that is also an
option. Do not open a public issue containing someone else's private details.

For software vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
